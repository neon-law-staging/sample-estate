prompts:
  estate_plan: >-
    Are your family details and inheritance wishes ready for counsel to review? Cover where you live and plan to sign,
    any planned move, your family and support obligations, existing estate documents and where the originals are kept,
    gifts and backup beneficiaries, any beneficiary who needs protection, guardians for minor children, and your wishes
    for your remains. Name your personal representative (executor), financial agent, health-care agent and a backup for
    each, once, through the Person records. Counsel records any trust goal, urgent concern or open decision in the
    private Matter file.
  trustee: >-
    Who will be the trustee of your revocable living trust? Most people name themselves. That leaves control exactly
    where it is now, and no one else has to sign the trust. If you name someone else, or a co-trustee, that person signs
    the trust too. They may sign at any time before your death, so they usually sign at the second sitting rather than
    the first. Either way, name your successor trustee through the Person records already used. A successor trustee
    signs nothing now.
  trust_terms: >-
    Are the rest of your trust instructions ready for counsel to review? Say who receives what is left after any
    specific gifts, and who takes a share if a beneficiary dies before you. List which assets will be retitled into the
    trust and which will stay outside it. A trust controls only the property actually transferred into it, and it saves
    no estate tax. What it offers is privacy: trust property passes without a public probate case. You sign the trust at
    or before the time you sign your will, never after.
  asset_schedule: >-
    Is your list of assets and debts ready for counsel to review? Cover bank and investment accounts, retirement
    accounts, insurance, real estate, personal property, businesses, equity, intellectual property and any
    cryptocurrency. For each one, record the owner, the capacity in which it is owned, your share, any co-owners, the
    custodian, its location, a dated value, debts, restrictions, deadlines, beneficiaries and what you want done with
    it. Include dated supporting records, and keep what you report from memory separate from what a record confirms.
    Note any title or beneficiary conflict and any follow-up task. This same list is reused for digital planning and any
    ownership attestation. Never send passwords or other credentials.
  financial_powers: >-
    Are your instructions for your financial agent ready for counsel to review? For the people already named, confirm
    that each is willing to serve, who the successors are and any conflicts of interest. Say when the agent's authority
    should begin, any event that must happen first and how that event will be proved, and whether co-agents must act
    together or may act separately. Cover financial, property, business, tax, retirement and digital powers; gifts;
    whether the agent may benefit personally; beneficiary changes; trusts; and any limits or oversight. Access to the
    content of your messages is asked separately below.
  health_directives: >-
    Are your health-care instructions ready for counsel to review? Name your health-care agent and alternate through the
    Person records already used, and confirm that neither will act as a witness. Your agent may not be your own health
    care provider, an employee of that provider, or the operator or an employee of a health-care facility, unless the
    agent is your spouse, legal guardian or next of kin. State your wishes about artificial nutrition and hydration
    (tube feeding) in your own words. If you sign a Nevada declaration and reach the end of life, tube feeding is
    withheld unless you have said otherwise in writing. Say whether you want a separate declaration, who may receive
    your medical records apart from your agent, who should order the disposition of your remains, and your organ, eye
    and tissue donation wishes. The power of attorney for health care is signed before a notary or two adult witnesses.
    It is a separate document and is never combined with the financial power of attorney.
  online_tools: >-
    Have you reviewed the online-tool settings (such as a legacy contact or inactive-account manager) for every account
    provider identified? For each tool, record the recipient, the disclosure settings, the date you checked, and whether
    you can still change or delete the direction, in the same private list. A direction you give through such a tool can
    override your will, trust or power of attorney, so counsel reviews these settings before drafting.
  lifetime_content: >-
    During your life, what access should your financial agent have to the content of your messages, such as emails and
    texts? Record any account-specific limits or exclusions privately. Counsel will match these instructions to the
    provider settings already recorded.
  death_content: >-
    After your death, should the content of your messages be disclosed? Record any account-specific limits or exclusions
    privately, including any difference from your answer for your lifetime.
  digital_continuity: >-
    Are your instructions for recovering and passing on your digital assets ready for counsel to review? Add to the same
    list how each account and digital asset should be accessed, preserved, closed or transferred, including access to
    the list of your messages without their content. Cover domains, renewals and the payments they depend on, email,
    devices, backups, hosting, code repositories, licenses, employer or co-author rights and who will maintain each
    project. Say who decides, who does the work and what would break first. Describe where your recovery instructions
    are kept, how an authorized fiduciary will find them and what you have tested. Never provide passwords, seed
    phrases, recovery codes or private keys.
  ownership_attestation: >-
    Do you want counsel to prepare a separate dated ownership statement with a lawyer's attestation? If yes, it reuses
    your asset list and records. Specify which interests it covers, the ownership date, time and time zone, any gaps in
    the evidence, any qualifications, and the role of any third signer. Discuss public-key readiness and privacy with
    counsel before anything is made public. This answer does not authorize publication. You and the lawyer must both
    sign the same final version, and any change needs fresh signatures. Proving that you hold a public key needs a
    separate verified challenge, and correction, replacement and lost-key handling each need instructions. No signature
    or blockchain step happens in this questionnaire.
  execution_plan: >-
    Is the signing arranged? Nevada allows electronic wills, but this plan is signed on paper and in person unless
    counsel agrees otherwise. You sign the trust at or before the will, because the will's gift to a trust that has not
    yet been signed can fail. The trust is acknowledged before a notary. The will needs two witnesses who take nothing
    under it, and their sworn affidavit is signed before a notary. The financial power of attorney, the certification of
    trust and the remains affidavit need a notary. The power of attorney for health care needs a notary or two adult
    witnesses. Tell counsel who can attend and whether anyone you have named is out of state.

# Combined estate-planning intake

This questionnaire collects your instructions for your Nevada estate plan in one place. Answer each topic once. Counsel
reviews every answer, follows up where needed, and then drafts the documents for you to review before anything is
signed. Your answers are not legal advice by themselves, and completing this form signs no document, confirms no
ownership, and authorizes nothing to be published. Answer "Not yet on file" when you do not know. Counsel records the
details and supporting records in the private Matter file, reusing the Person records and one asset list. Never provide
passwords or other credentials.
